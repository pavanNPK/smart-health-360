Pharmacy Payment & Medicine Sales Application

A branch-ready pharmacy billing, payment, inventory, invoice, and reporting platform for physical stores first, with a future path to online medicine selling and mobile apps.

Current goal: maintain pharmacy billing and stock across many branches with one central system.
Physical store POS Payments Inventory Invoices Reports Future online sales
Business Problem

Why This Platform Is Needed

02

Billing Accuracy

Reduce billing mistakes, wrong discounts, manual tax errors, and missing invoice records.

Branch Stock Control

Track every medicine by branch, batch, expiry date, rack, and available quantity.

Payment Visibility

Connect cash, UPI, card, wallet, refunds, and daily closing reports.

Central Reporting

Owners and managers can see branch sales, stock, payments, and exceptions centrally.

Audit Trail

Know who changed stock, price, invoice, discount, refund, or branch settings.

Future Ready

The same platform can later support online ordering, mobile app, prescription upload, and delivery.

Architecture

Best Starting Strategy

08

Start With Modular Monolith

Build one clean Spring Boot application with separated modules. This is faster, cheaper, easier to test, and better for early business changes.

Angular Web AppPOS + admin + reports
Spring Boot APIAuth, products, inventory, billing, payments
PostgreSQLMain transactional database
Redis + S3Cache and file storage
Payment GatewayUPI, card, wallet, online payment

Split Later Only When Needed

  • Auth Service
  • Product Service
  • Inventory Service
  • Billing Service
  • Payment Service
  • Report Service
  • Notification Service

Development Roadmap

Build In Phases

10

Phase 1: Physical Store MVP

Login, roles, branches, medicine master, inventory, barcode billing, payments, invoice print, basic reports.

Phase 2: Store Operations

Returns, refunds, supplier purchase, stock transfer, low stock alerts, expiry alerts, audit logs, prescription upload.

Phase 3: Multi-Branch Scale

Central dashboard, regional reports, reconciliation, GST reports, advanced inventory, Redis, queue-based reports.

Phase 4: Online Medicine Selling

Customer web app, mobile app, medicine search, prescription upload, online payment, delivery, pickup, order tracking.
